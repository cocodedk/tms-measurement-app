Mark the Beam F3 distances

Enter the three head measurements first. Then use the calculated distances to mark the site. The images below come from the Beam F3 guide, which also labels a distance Z. The app calculates X, Y and adjusted Y, not Z.

1 X distance from midline

Mark the X cm distance from the midline across the forehead.

2 Y distance from the vertex

From the vertex (Cz), measure Y cm along the scalp toward the forehead mark.

3 Z distance at the side of the head (reference only)

Reference diagram: Z joins the two marked points on the side of the head. This app does not calculate Z.

Locate F3

The app calculates X and Y from your measurements and applies the Beam F3 adjustment. Use the steps below to mark the site.

X = 0.1154 × HC   |   Y = 0.2637 × ((TTT + NI) / 2)   |   Yadj = Y + 0.35 cm
  • Measure X along the circumference from the midline toward the left side.
  • From Cz, follow the scalp arc toward the forehead mark made with X.
  • Measure Yadj along that arc to locate the F3 point.

Reference map

The EEG reference map helps confirm the F3 region relative to the 10-20 system. Use it alongside the calculated distances for placement.

The app stores every measurement locally, so clinicians can review previous sessions without network access.

Track treatment sessions

Record each TMS session under its measurement: stimulation intensity, pulses, frequency, and more. Every session is saved locally on the device, so you can review a client's full treatment history offline.

  • Intensity (% of motor threshold, MT) and the motor threshold (% of maximum stimulator output, MSO)
  • Total pulses, frequency (Hz), and the train breakdown (trains, pulses per train, durations)
  • Session date, stimulation site, and free-text notes — editable any time
