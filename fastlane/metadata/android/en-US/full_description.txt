TMS Measurement App is an open-source, offline tool for calculating Beam F3 targeting distances used in transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) treatment planning.

Enter three head measurements to compute the X, Y, and adjusted Y distances for F3 site localization, then save the results per client for follow-up sessions.

Each client record can also store a log of treatment sessions, including intensity, pulse count, frequency, motor threshold, train parameters, site, and notes.

The app requests no permissions, has no internet access, and does not collect, transmit, or share any data. All measurements and session logs stay on the device.

The interface is available in English, Persian, Arabic, Traditional Chinese, French, Spanish, German, and Japanese.
